THE STANDARD / 05 PRINCIPLES

THE RULES OF INTEGRITY.

How to find, evaluate, use, and share information responsibly.

01

RESPECT INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

OWNERSHIP / CREDIT

Every original work carries the rights of its creator. Acknowledge authorship, seek permission when required, and follow applicable copyright rules before reusing another person’s work.

SOURCE 01 ↗
02

CITE RELIABLE SOURCES

EVIDENCE / TRACEABILITY

Give readers a clear path to the evidence behind your claims. Record the author, title, publication details, and link; accurate citations strengthen credibility and let others verify your work.

SOURCE 02 ↗
03

VERIFY INFORMATION

ACCURACY / FACT-CHECK

Check the source, date, author, evidence, and purpose before accepting a claim. Cross-check it with independent, reputable sources; urgency and popularity are never proof of truth.

SOURCE 03 ↗
04

PROTECT PRIVACY

DIGNITY / SECURITY

Personal information belongs to the person it describes. Limit what you disclose, secure accounts and location settings, and obtain consent before sharing someone else’s private details or images.

SOURCE 04 ↗
05

PRACTICE RESPONSIBLE SHARING

IMPACT / DIGITAL CITIZENSHIP

Pause before you post: consider accuracy, audience, consent, and possible consequences. Communicate lawfully, safely, and respectfully—because digital actions can affect real people and reputations.
